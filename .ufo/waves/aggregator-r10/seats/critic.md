Explicit `--repos`, `--repo`, `--snapshot`, existing grading rules, frozen expectations, and exit precedence remain unchanged. This covers the enumerated local directory set, not missing configured paths, an atomic/frozen fleet state, independent promotion review, or complete semantic CORPUS conformance.
