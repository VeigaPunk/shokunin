Gate (must run on the frozen counter, on committed bytes, before report filing):

1. **Forgery battery must be fully refused.** Freeze is void unless a committed battery — criterion-111 family (fingerprint, invocation-sequence, pinned-hash, always-accept) *plus* subject-shaped counterfeits (label/basename oracle, worktree/hybrid, cited-tree pin, prose citation, parked/stub stale-runner, duplicate-base, alias hardlink/dirswap/symlink, counterfeit witness with no subprocess, `refs/replace` env, dup-verdict row, print-only registry row, wrong-count correction, neutered checker) — all print `FAIL`/non-`PASS` by the *named* conjunct each.
