## 1. Method (binding, per the operator directive)

- **TTFT** = time from request send to the first decoded content token on a streaming request. Fixed identical prompt across every model (the same prompt as TPS), `max_tokens` 256, temperature 0 where the lane accepts it.
- **TPS** = decode throughput excluding TTFT: `(completion_tokens − 1) / (last-delta time − first-delta time)`, with `completion_tokens` taken from the server's `usage` block. The span starts at the first delta of any kind (a thinking lane that streams reasoning first would otherwise be credited with throughput it did not spend on output); the headline TTFT column is content-only, and the reasoning TTFT is reported separately.
- **n = 2 reps per model, sequential**; models run with bounded concurrency so no lane's own concurrency is a variable.
- **Burst rule**: a decode span under 50 ms with deltas present means the lane flushed the whole answer in one burst. TPS is reported as not derivable rather than as a throughput number, and such lanes are excluded from TPS rankings.
- **One attempt on a dead lane**: auth/quota walls and refused request shapes abort the model immediately; two consecutive transport failures abort it too. The remainder is recorded as `skipped_after_fatal`.
- **Failure-class taxonomy**: transport / auth / refusal / truncation / format_break / wrong_answer, plus `skipped_after_fatal`. A `request_level` failure is one the lane is answerable for; a content-level failure is about one answer.
- **Codex lanes** use the M1 D6 shape (`store:false`, `stream:true`, list `input`, no `temperature`, `max_output_tokens >= 16`); **devin-class lanes** get one warm-up pre-call excluded from every metric.

## 8. Routing implications

## 9. Limits and open questions

- **The budget is the battery's, not the model's.** Every thinking lane that truncated did so inside 192 output tokens. A longer budget would let those lanes finish; the battery reports the truncation because the measurement contract fixed the budget, and a lane that needs more than 192 tokens of reasoning before a one-line answer is slow by construction for short subtasks.
- **n = 2 reps** bounds the confidence interval for TTFT/TPS to roughly "two draws agree or not". Lanes whose two reps differ by more than a few hundred ms are flagged by the rep column rather than smoothed over.
- **Fixed small prompt.** TTFT here is dominated by connection, queueing and prefill for a ~50-token prompt; a long-context lane may rank differently at real prompt sizes.
- **One correctness draw per probe per model** (temperature 0 where accepted). A wrong answer is one sample, not a rate; the pass counts are a signal about format discipline and basic competence, not a benchmark score.
- **Burst lanes carry no TPS.** Lanes whose shim buffers the whole response cannot be ranked on throughput from a client-side measurement; that is a property of the transport, and the fix (incremental flushing in the shim) is outside this mission's scope.
- **Reasoning tokens interact with small `max_tokens`.** Thin caps can be consumed by thinking before any content is emitted, which shows up as `truncation`; where that happened the probe spent 192 tokens, and the failure is reported as what it is rather than retried with a bigger budget.
- **Not covered here**: tool-call *correctness* beyond one well-formedness probe, multimodal input, effort-parameter behaviour, long-context behaviour, and anything about lanes the pool does not assign.
